Why I Created DearMe

I built DearMe as a student web development project because I wanted to create a peaceful, aesthetic, and safe online corner for teenagers. Sometimes school, friends, and life can feel overwhelming, and having a personal space to write, process emotions, and slow down can make a big difference.

What I Learned

While building this project, I learned how to work with HTML, CSS grid and flexbox, theme variables, dynamic DOM manipulation, and local browser storage (localStorage) to keep user data private!

🔐 A Note on Privacy:

DearMe stores all diary entries, letters, and mood history locally on your device using your browser's LocalStorage. None of your private thoughts are sent to any external server.
